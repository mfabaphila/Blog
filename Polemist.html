The Boken Healer-Phila Mfaba

**A true story: A polemist seed**

Many, many moons ago, way before someone ate the apple, a bamboo seed was buried along with a polemist seed. The bamboo seed sat in obedient silence; it drowned in humble silence in its pool of waste, dirt, and stench.

The polemist seed had had it. It shouted, “God, why do you sit us under these conditions? Why do we suffer in this pit, dark as night itself? Why do you allow our existence to be covered in feces, sewage, and all sorts of waste? What evil deed led to this barbarity? We see no light, we breathe no clean air, we feel no heat of the sun and gentle morning breeze. We are treated far worse than feces.

“We demand to be treated as cows that bathe in your warm sun; we demand to chirp and peck around a gust of clean breeze like Aves do.”

God listened attentively. He put down a clay structure of Adam. He looked at the polemist seed and said, “Fair enough. And what about you, bamboo seed?” he asked.

The bamboo seed did not hesitate to respond. It said, “I will wait on your call, my Creator.”

God said, “Well then.” He let the polemist seed out of the soil.

The polemist seed was happy. It felt the fleeting breeze whooshing over its skin; it dined in the warm summer sun. It inhaled a ball of fresh air below a tree.

It whistled as it pranced with joy. It waved and smiled as it passed a field of lavender wildflowers; they saluted it by swaying gently back and forth in the breeze.

“Beautiful,” it said. It saluted a pond of clear, calm water.

“Look at all this beauty that I had been missing.” A thought wafted along its head, along with a smear of morning gust. It saw animals go on with their lives. A flock of starlings murmured. It saw a herd of wildebeest ripple momentarily, veering left and right as one.

“Ahh,” exclaimed the little polemist seed under an orange twilight. “Hello there, sun,” it said. It approached a flock of chickens pecking. A chicken ate the polemist seed. Its life ended.

A few months later, a bamboo tree grew. It grew up to 20 metres tall, and it enjoyed the sun and breeze and all the scenery of life to infinity.

The story of the polemist seed carries an important lesson about patience and trusting the process. The most important lesson, though, is that you don't mess with a chicken.
