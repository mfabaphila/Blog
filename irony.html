10 Ironies That Will Help You from Being a Slay Queen

The overused maxim, "life is not black and white," means that in most situations, things are not as clear as they look.

My personal philosophy is similar, but a bit better and unique. I hold that life’s true meaning lies in the things that we shun the most.

Some people believe that moderation is a way to a better life. I don’t dispute that, but I think a way to an even greater life is to seek the opposite of what you would like to manifest.

If you think about sleeping when you want to sleep, you might be an owl for that night. It's like focusing too much on orgasm; it goes away.

So here is a list of my contradicting life lessons for an even greater life experience. I call them "10 ironies that will help you from being a slay queen or a cool kid."

1. Seeking pleasure yields to pain and suffering.

2. The first step to abundance is emptiness—give to receive.

3. A good company is found in isolation.

4. In a world of 7 billion people, only you can save you from you.

5. Love is what you give when falling in love fades away.

6. If you want life to be easy on you, accept the fact that life is hard.

7. Pain is necessary for joy and growth.

8. You start by changing the way you look at life; then life will follow your perception.

9. All that ends well starts bad.

10. Slay queens and cool kids are fun but expensive.
